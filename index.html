How progression works

1. Hit the top of the range

For example, complete 10 reps on all 3 sets of a 6–10 rep exercise with clean form.

2. Increase the load

Try the smallest practical jump, often 2.5–5 lb for upper-body lifts or 5–10 lb for lower-body lifts.

3. Build reps again

Expect reps to drop toward the bottom of the range after increasing weight. Work them back up.
